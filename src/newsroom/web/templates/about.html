About

This site lists news headlines from a fixed set of outlets and shows, for each outlet, who owns it and, where a public record exists, how it is funded. Every ownership or funding statement links to the record it comes from. Nothing is inferred, estimated, rated or scored.

What the labels mean

Not publicly disclosed
None of the public sources this site uses has a record for this. For ownership, the outlet could not be matched to a Wikidata item. For funding, no filing, registry entry or published figure was found for the outlet or any of its owners. It is a statement about the records available here, not a claim about the outlet.
No owner recorded
The outlet has a Wikidata item, but that item states no current "owned by" (P127) or "parent organization" (P749). This is common for independent nonprofits and for organizations no other entity owns. It can also mean the record is incomplete.
Owned by / Parent organization
Shown exactly as Wikidata records them: "owned by" (P127) and "parent organization" (P749) are different statements and are labelled separately. A percentage appears only when Wikidata states the share (P1107).
Ultimately
The top of the recorded chain: the entity or entities above the outlet for which no further owner is recorded.

Articles

Headlines come from the GDELT Project's DOC 2.0 API, queried every {{ interval }} minutes for each outlet on the list (GDELT itself updates every 15 minutes). Only metadata is kept: headline, link, outlet, language and date. No article text is stored. Links go to the original outlet. Times are in {{ request.app.state.settings.timezone }}. The same article under slightly different links (tracking parameters, "www.", trailing slashes) is shown once. Topic tags come from a published keyword list matched against headlines, not from any judgement about content.

Dates

GDELT does not report when an article was published, only when it first saw it. So shortly after each new article is collected, the site reads its publication time from the article page's own metadata, the same tags search engines and link previews use: schema.org datePublished, then article:published_time, then a few other standard date tags. Only the date is kept. A date is used only if it includes a time zone, is not later than when GDELT saw the article, and is after 1995.

  • Published … is the time the article page gives.
  • Seen … means the page gave no usable date (or has not been checked yet), so the time GDELT first saw the article is shown instead. This can be minutes to days after publication.

To be a considerate visitor, the site reads each page at most twice, only for articles from about the last day, at most one request per second and a limited number per pass, and it follows each site's robots.txt. A site that asks it to slow down is left alone until the next pass. The feed is sorted and grouped by the date shown.

Ownership

Each outlet's web domain is matched to a Wikidata item through that item's "official website" (P856). If several items claim the same website, or none does, the match is made by hand; the outlet page says how each match was made. From the outlet's item, the site follows "owned by" and "parent organization" upward, up to ten levels. Only statements Wikidata marks as current are used: deprecated statements, statements with an end date in the past, and "unknown value" statements are ignored; where Wikidata marks one statement as preferred, it is used over the others. If the records loop back on themselves, the loop is shown and not followed. Ownership is re-checked weekly. Occasionally a link missing from Wikidata is added by hand; it is labelled "added manually" and links to its own public source.

Funding

Funding records are attached to the outlet or to any owner above it, and are shown only where a public record exists:

  • Public companies: links to the latest annual report (Form 10-K, 20-F or 40-F) and the full filing index on SEC EDGAR. Figures are not extracted from filings; a company's revenue is not the same thing as funding.
  • US nonprofits: total revenue and "contributions, gifts and grants" for the last three tax years, as reported on IRS Form 990, via ProPublica's Nonprofit Explorer.
  • Canadian registered charities: a link to the charity's listing with the Canada Revenue Agency, which publishes its annual T3010 returns.
  • Public broadcasters and other published figures: copied as stated from an annual report or government document, each with a link and the date it was checked.

Anything else is shown as "Not publicly disclosed".

What this site does not do

  • No bias, reliability or "trust" ratings, and no sentiment or other scores.
  • No commentary on outlets, owners or articles.
  • No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no cookies, and no third-party requests from your browser.
  • No article text or article images are stored or shown.
