About

This site lists headlines from a set of news outlets and shows who owns each outlet and how it's funded. Every ownership and funding detail links to the public record it comes from. Nothing is rated, scored or guessed.

What the labels mean

Not publicly disclosed
No public record was found. This says nothing about the outlet itself.
No owner recorded
The outlet has a Wikidata entry, but it doesn't list an owner. This is common for independent and nonprofit outlets, but the entry may also be incomplete.
Owned by / Parent organization
Two different Wikidata statements, shown as recorded. A percentage appears only when the record gives one.
Ultimately
The top of the ownership chain, as far as the records go.
Published / Seen
Published is the time given on the article page. Seen is when the article was first picked up. It's shown when the page gives no date, and can be later than the real publication time.

Articles

Headlines come from the GDELT Project and, for outlets GDELT doesn't carry, from the outlet's own RSS feed. Both are checked every {{ interval }} minutes. Only the headline, link, outlet, language and date are kept, never the article text. Links go straight to the outlet. Times are in {{ request.app.state.settings.timezone }}. Topic tags are based on keywords in the headline. A few outlets are in neither source; their page says so.

Some outlets publish far more than others, often because they also run wire stories. So the feed shows at most {{ cap }} articles per outlet per hour, and tells you how many it left out. Choose Show: Everything to see them all. An outlet's own page and the outlet filter always show everything.

Dates

An outlet's RSS feed states when each article was published, and that time is used. GDELT only records when it found an article, so for those the site checks each new article's page for its publication date, the same date search engines use. Only the date is kept. New articles appear once their date has been checked, usually within a minute of being collected, so they show the right time from the start. Each page is checked at most twice, and each site's robots.txt is respected.

A date isn't used if it's later than when the article was first picked up, or if the page's own date tags contradict each other about the time zone. Those articles show when they were seen instead.

Ownership

Each outlet is matched to its Wikidata entry by its website, and the outlet page says how the match was made. From there the site follows "owned by" and "parent organization" upward, up to ten levels. Only current records are used, and ownership is rechecked weekly. Any link added by hand is labelled and cites its own source.

Funding

Shown only where a public record exists:

  • Public companies: their latest annual report and filings on SEC EDGAR. No figures are taken from these, because a company's revenue isn't funding.
  • US nonprofits: revenue and donations from IRS Form 990 filings, via ProPublica.
  • Canadian charities: their listing with the Canada Revenue Agency.
  • Public broadcasters and others: figures as published in annual reports or government documents.

What this site doesn't do

  • No bias or reliability ratings, and no scores of any kind.
  • No commentary.
  • No accounts, tracking, analytics or cookies. Your browser only connects to this site.
  • No article text or images.
